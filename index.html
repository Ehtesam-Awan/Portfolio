01 / About

Two disciplines, one instinct for making things clearer.

I started building technical and creative skills during my college years — not in a straight line, but in the way most useful skills actually arrive: through teaching, graphic design, content creation, and digital media, often at the same time.

Standing in front of a classroom taught me how to explain a hard idea simply. Designing for real clients taught me how to make that idea look inevitable. Somewhere in between, I picked up the habits that connect the two — clear communication, steady leadership, and a comfort with problems that don't have an obvious first step.

I stayed active in university societies and academic circles, which is where the confidence and public-speaking side of my work really took shape. What I bring to a project now is that same combination: education, creativity, and technology, moving together instead of in separate lanes.
